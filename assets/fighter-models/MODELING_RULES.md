# Fighter model customization rule

Base fighter and equipment models must not contain baked faction symbols,
emblems, lettering, heraldry, or other customization marks.

Keep customizable surfaces as clean geometry with an approved neutral material
slot. Colors, symbols, wear patterns, and progression rewards are applied later
through textures and runtime material customization.

This applies to shields, armor plates, clothing panels, weapon faces, banners,
and every future fighter asset.
